Which guidance style?

A

Follow the drop (recommended)

A drop tours each screen, lingering briefly in the corners, so your head and eyes cover the whole screen without you having to think about it. It gives the most complete and reliable calibration.

B

Look around freely

A card on each screen says "look around this whole screen" with a 4-second countdown ring. Simpler, but people tend to stare at the middle, which gives the calibration less to work with.

Rules behind it
  • It never takes focus: the overlays and cards are click-through, like the dock. Esc cancels at any time; the Raw Input watcher sees the key without capturing it.
  • It only counts what it can see: samples are recorded only while your face is detected. If it loses you, the drop waits and the card says "Can't see you. Is the room too dark?"
  • An honest result: the separation figure measures how far apart your two looking positions are compared with how much they wobble. Below 2σ it says "Too close. Turn your head a little more, or move the LG closer to the laptop." and offers Redo.
  • Saved per monitor layout: it's stored with the monitors' IDs and positions. If you move, add or remove a monitor, the dock shows a small "!" and asks you to recalibrate. It never keeps running on a stale calibration.
